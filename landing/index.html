Two halves of one shift

The counter, and the crew behind it.

A cafe is a room where stock turns into sales and people turn into wages. Software that only watches the first half can tell you what sold, never what it cost you to sell it.

Live today

The counter

Billing that keeps up with the 8:40 rush, and a kitchen that hears the order without anyone shouting.

  • Menu — price, GST slab, HSN, veg flag and availability, per item
  • Orders and KOT — dine-in, takeaway and delivery; a kitchen screen that sorts by who has waited longest
  • GST-correct bills — line items, CGST/SGST per slab, discount applied before tax, service charge, round-off
  • Part payment and voids — cash, UPI or card, and nothing is cancelled without a reason against a name
  • Day close — revenue read from the payments actually taken, never typed in
Live today

The crew

The part every other cafe POS leaves in a WhatsApp group and a paper register.

  • Split-shift attendance — two sessions a day, check-in and check-out, the way a cafe actually staffs
  • Salary structure with PF & ESI — package, monthly, allowances, deductions, held per employee
  • Incentives and increments — daily, monthly, annual or performance, with the history behind each raise
  • Documents on file — the paperwork you scramble for at inspection, filed against the person
  • Performance reviews — so a raise is a record, not an argument
Where the margin goes

You know what sold. Do you know what it cost?

Most cafes price a cappuccino once, in year one, and never touch it again — while milk, beans and wages all move. The chain below is the whole point of putting stock and staff in one system.

01RecipeEach menu item knows what goes into it, down to the gram.
02SaleA bill line depletes those ingredients automatically. No stock-taking evening.
03VarianceWhat the recipe says you used versus what the shelf says. That gap is wastage or theft.
04True marginIngredient cost plus the labour hours that shift, against the price on the board.

The end of that chain is one sentence you can act on: this drink makes money, this one does not, and here is by how much.
